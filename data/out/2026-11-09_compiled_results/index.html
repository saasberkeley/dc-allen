Main findings

Across all five analyses
  • Mishler's rules find few motifs here. On PL5 the strict rules find one awake motif and nothing under isoflurane; the relaxed version adds one per state. Across channels, strict motifs appear only while awake (11 of 45 LFP regions, 9 of 26 EEG channels); under isoflurane only the relaxed version finds any (17 LFP, 21 EEG).
  • Awake and isoflurane motifs are different patterns. Bootstrap resamples agree within a state (median |r| 0.97) but not across states (0.37). On PL5 the awake motif is PC1 of the awake data (|r| 0.99); the isoflurane motif is PC2 of the isoflurane data (|r| 1.00).
  • In spontaneous activity, a motif is a direction through one continuous cloud, not two discrete states. Spontaneous awake shows one hump on every motif axis and one Gaussian-mixture component in 10-D. The + and − poles are the two tails of that hump.
  • The discrete structure in this recording is states and events: awake vs isoflurane, stimulus blocks, and a broadband-low "suppression" population of seconds under isoflurane (about 8–10% of spontaneous isoflurane seconds).
  • Candidate count can grow about 26× (48 → 1,238 distinct across 45 LFP regions) by keeping every candidate with its p-value as a score and detecting in finer time bins. The union across channels never levels off, so most extra candidates are probably channel-specific; a cross-channel recurrence score is needed to rank them.
  • Gaussian mixtures label states well but don't find new spontaneous motifs. With 2 components the poles are always exact mirror images, a consequence of z-scoring. With 3+ components they can differ, but the difference is mostly an overall broadband level.
  • Spikes already separate by pole while awake. PL5 units fire 0.94 z higher in the + pole than in the − pole (p = 0.001, strict motif); under isoflurane the difference is 0.64 z (p = 0.07).
  • Confound to keep in mind: the awake + pole concentrates in the visual-stimulus block (82% of high-scoring seconds in 36% of the time; about 50% of its seconds in 17% of the time with the EDA bands). Visual stimulation and time in session can't be separated in this session.
  • Behaviour: motif or component expression tracks pupil size (r = 0.50–0.60, largely from the stretch before induction); running barely (|r| ≤ 0.14).

Checks on the method

  • Calibration on synthetic noise (20 datasets each): independent bands give 1 false motif under strict, 0 under relaxed. A shared broadband component produces false motifs under every version (16/20 relaxed at weight 0.5), but 15 of 16 are caught by the broadband flag (at most 1 band of the minority sign).
  • Null-size bias: circular shifts keep 59–92% more seconds than real data under isoflurane (e.g. 1,897 vs 1,191 for EEG ch13), because real isoflurane has more all-same-sign seconds that get dropped. This inflates the null; the relaxed version normalises for it.
  • Bootstrap over chunks (40 resamples): PL5 detection 0.88 awake, 0.93 isoflurane; EEG 0.28–0.68.
  • Exact permutation test of chunks: pole balance differs between states (p = 0.008 for PL5 and EEG ch13); motif shape does not differ significantly (p = 0.11, 0.40).
  • EEG vs LFP: isoflurane motifs match in shape (|r| 0.93 ch13, 0.91 ch10) and in time (r = 0.68).

Band-count EDA: sub-poles and baseline

8 Nov · band_eda.ipynb · PL5 · 5, 6, 8, 10, 20 log-spaced bands (not the report set) · awake, isoflurane, full · stimuli included or excluded
  • Single bands: awake is one slightly right-skewed hump per band. Isoflurane with stimuli adds a sharp low peak in every band (suppression seconds).
  • PCA: awake is one cloud; isoflurane a fan with a dense tip; full two clusters. Uncentered PC1 holds 93–100% of the variance and is the average spectrum.
  • Sub-poles across the axis (split inside a pole): 7 of 84 poles at p ≤ 0.05, about 4 expected by chance. The only repeatable one is 99% electrical stimulation and disappears with stimuli excluded.
  • Bimodality along the axis (dip test vs. band-shifted data): 6 of 11 axes at 10 bands. A stricter surrogate later showed most of this is states, stimulation or suppression (see humps).
  • Baseline: with stimuli excluded, quiet seconds are 1.2–1.5× more common than in a matched Gaussian under isoflurane (p ≈ 0.005 at every band count) and 1.2–2.0× awake (significant at 5–8 bands). No separate central cluster.

Gaussian mixtures in the full 10-band space

9 Nov · gmm_motifs.ipynb · PL5 · report bands · number of components chosen on held-out time against phase-randomised surrogates

Each second is a point in 10-D; a mixture is fitted with no motif axis given. Component means are candidate motifs; responsibilities are their expression. "Extra structure" is how much more the real data gains from extra components than surrogates with the same band correlations but no clusters (held-out log-likelihood, nats per second).

  • No axis shows the strong/weak pattern on both sides. Spontaneous awake has one hump on both axes.
  • The one spontaneous extra hump (isoflurane M1) is broadband-low suppression seconds: a separate state, not a stronger version of M1.
  • The band-shift null used in the EDA was too weak: it breaks band correlations. Against the stricter surrogate, the remaining bimodality is states, stimulation, or suppression.
  • Open test, needs spikes: bin each axis's projection into 5 strength levels and plot firing against strength. Monotonic firing means one motif with a continuous strength; non-monotonic firing would justify sub-motifs even without humps.

Caveats

  • One mouse, one session. Most EDA and the mixture work use one channel (PL5).
  • Ten bands throughout, not Mishler's 76 bands [k, k+4]; no 60 Hz notch or noise filter. The band EDA used log-spaced bands, not the report set.
  • Spontaneous bins are short (947 s awake, 1,388 s isoflurane), which limits how many components or humps any test can support.
  • Stimulus blocks are fixed in time, so stimulus effects and time-in-session effects are confounded.
  • No held-out validation has been run yet; the spike result above comes from the same data the motif was found on.
